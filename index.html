Privacy Policy: Orbit Dodge

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Orbit Dodge ("the app") is a game published by xOrbit Studio.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Your scores, coins, unlocked skins, missions and settings are stored locally on your device only and are not sent anywhere.

Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded video ads, which you can choose to watch in return for in-game coins. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, IP address and usage and diagnostic data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

The app does not include any other third-party analytics services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion

Because the app stores nothing on our servers, uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
