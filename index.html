Privacy Policy: Orbit Dodge

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Orbit Dodge ("the app") is a game published by xOrbit Studio.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Your scores, coins, unlocked skins, missions and settings are stored locally on your device only and are not sent anywhere.

Advertising and third parties

The app does not show ads and does not include any third-party analytics or advertising services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion

Because the app stores nothing on our servers, uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.
